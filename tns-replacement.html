How the new combination compares

Functionalab EXO-GF

EXO-GF is the closest replacement for the regenerative role of TNS Advanced+. TNS Advanced+ uses growth factors, peptides and supporting ingredients to improve the appearance of firmness, fine lines, tone and texture.

EXO-GF takes a different and more advanced biotechnology approach by combining exosomes, growth factors and NAD⁺ to support skin renewal, firmness, density and overall skin rejuvenation.

SkinBetter AlphaRet

SkinMedica Retinol Complex uses retinol with niacinamide, squalane, ceramides, antioxidants and peptides.

AlphaRet uses a proprietary combination of a retinoid and AHA, a peptide blend, ceramides, squalane, niacinamide, hyaluronic acid, and antioxidants including vitamins C and E and CoQ10.

This makes AlphaRet a more comprehensive formulation: it combines retinoid renewal, AHA exfoliation, peptide support, hydration and barrier support, and antioxidant protection in one product. The advantage is not simply that AlphaRet is "stronger" — it's that SkinBetter has developed a more sophisticated delivery and formulation approach to balance efficacy and tolerability, making it an excellent option for consistent, long-term anti-aging use.

Not a Like-for-Like Replacement

A more comprehensive & advanced approach

The new routine is not simply a like-for-like replacement. Because these are different technologies, we wouldn't describe the new combination as a direct ingredient-for-ingredient equivalent. Instead, we consider it a more comprehensive and technologically advanced anti-aging approach — addressing both regenerative support and active skin renewal.

Why this combination works

  • Regenerative support: EXO-GF's exosome, growth factor and NAD⁺ technology supports skin renewal, firmness and density.
  • Active renewal: AlphaRet's retinoid / AHA formulation supports cell turnover and smoother texture.
  • Peptide + antioxidant support: Additional peptides, vitamins C & E, and CoQ10 help protect and support the skin.
  • Hydration & barrier support: Ceramides, squalane, niacinamide and hyaluronic acid help maintain a healthy barrier.
  • Balanced for long-term use: A sophisticated delivery and formulation approach balances efficacy with tolerability.

We are making the change because TNS Advanced+ is no longer readily available in Canada, and this gives us an opportunity to move you to an equally targeted — and in some aspects more advanced — skincare combination.

EXO-GF + AlphaRet would be an excellent next step for maintaining and further supporting your skin's firmness, texture, tone and overall rejuvenated appearance.
